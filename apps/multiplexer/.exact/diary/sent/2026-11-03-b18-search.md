surfaces: ios
agent: Claude Code, Claude Opus 5.5
task: a search across all of a chat app's chats, opened from the app's own floating bottom bar: the field at the bottom with the keyboard up, recent items before typing, results grouped by kind while typing, and a tap that opens an item's screen at the found message

### Rough
- A route header's `input type="search"` became UIKit's search field at the bottom of the screen on iOS 26.5 (a phone, no tab bar), with UIKit's round close button beside it when active: the look the app wanted. But the design needs the close button to end the search and go back to the list in one tap, and Exact passes no close event: closing gives the field's `input` with "" and a `blur`, which a scroll that puts the keyboard away also gives (with an empty field). A drive could also not press the field (`tap` answered "bar item #7 is disabled or presses nothing"; `type` worked) nor UIKit's close button, so the close could not be tested either. Drew the field at the bottom in the app's own glass bar instead, with an `input type="search"` and an app-drawn close button. About 20 minutes (one probe build and three drives). What would have helped: a `cancel` event on a header search field (UIKit's `searchBarCancelButtonClicked` or `didDismissSearchController`), a way to put a search button in a bottom bar that expands into the field (iOS 26's `searchBarPlacementBarButtonItem`), and a drive op that presses a header field's close button.
- `agent ios` has no `wait` op ("unknown op: wait"); `clock 1500` is the way to let a native animation finish. About 1 minute.
- `display=(searching ? "none" : "flex")` on a `list`, to hide it while another view covered it and keep its scroll place, laid the list's rows out side by side, as a flex row, when the value was "flex": the list stopped being a list of cells and nothing warned. About 5 minutes (a drive and a side-by-side screenshot). Used `opacity` and `inert` instead, which keep the list as it was. What would have helped: a warning that `display` on a `list` changes how its rows are laid out, or a way to hide a box and keep its state (`hidden`, `visibility`).
- A data-module source that marked the found words in 60 messages' text (a Unicode `normalize` of each character) took 272 ms of its turn, over the 100 ms budget, and was refused. On screen the resource just kept its previous answer: an empty page, no error, no spinner left. The refusal was only in `logs` ("`loadSearch` took 272.0 ms of its own (CPU) time ... over the 100 ms budget"), which then said exactly what to do. About 10 minutes (two drives before reading the logs). Fixed by folding only non-ASCII characters and only at word starts. What would have helped: the drive's step output, or `state <resource>`, naming a refused answer, and a note in the data-module docs that a turn has 100 ms of CPU and that the engine is slower than a browser's.
- A comment line between an `if` and the first line of its body ("`if` needs a non-empty indented body"), at the body's indentation, ended the block. About 2 minutes. What would have helped: comments allowed there, or the message saying the comment line is the cause.
- A `row` inside a list's `section` (a cell) has a gap of 8 and padding of 16 from its tag, so a photo grid of three tiles at 33.333% wrapped to two. `layout found-grid` showed the tag's values at once. About 5 minutes. What would have helped: the list docs saying a cell's own row brings gap and padding.
- `sharedElement=` is one line in the motion recipe ("a node flies from the old one's place"). A button in one component (the bar) and a field in another (the search bar), swapped by an `if`/`else`, with the same `sharedElement`, showed no flight in a filmed drive (`screenshot ... over 600 every 70 window`), and the docs do not say what a flight needs (same parent, same commit, same component). About 10 minutes. Used a keyframe (a slide from the button's side and a fade) instead. A per-axis `scale` for a widening bar is not built; the compiler said so clearly and named the alternative.
- `clock 3000 real` was refused ("not a time"); the form is `clock +3000 real`. About 1 minute.

### Lean in
- One header line (`input type="search"`) was enough to see UIKit's iOS 26 bottom search field, with `--chrome platform` and `screenshot … window` showing it as a person sees it.
- `lower-list-section` named the exact fix for sections in a list (`appearance="auto"`, `listStyle="plain"`, every child in a section), and the result was the platform's plain list with its section headers and separators.
- A `members` file kept the root's diff small again: the search's state, resource, mutation, tasks and actions are in their own file; the root changed in a `use` line, a `members` line, the inbox route's bar and content, and two lines of the chat's transcript.
- `check` at the largest text size passed the new screen after two fixes it made visible in screenshots; `screenshot ... over ... every ...` filmed the entrance, frame by frame.
- `inputmode="text"` on a search field gave the plain keyboard with the Search key (the default for `type="search"` is the web's address keyboard, with "." and an arrow), and `enterkeyhint="search"` labelled the key.
