### Rough
- The `audio` element's props and events are not on its vocab card: `contract vocab audio` says only "tag, Video (html); sets semanticTag=audio; positional src". `playbackRate`, `preservesPitch`, `paused` and `timeupdate` each answer by name, but the list of what the element takes (and that an `audio` without `controls` draws no box, so a custom player is a hidden `audio` and the app's own buttons) is only in the video LLP (§3, §8). About 5 minutes of grepping. What would have helped: the card listing the media node's props and events once, or a recipe for "a custom audio player" (play/pause, progress, speed) pointing at `paused`, `timeupdate`, `ended` and `playbackRate`.
- A refused permission ran no `then`. The composer kept its own steps ("opening" while `getUserMedia(audio=true)` asks), moved on by the request's `then`, as the permissions recipe and the recorder sample show (`then arrived` with a `case none`). A drive that denied the permission left it in "opening"; the agent's note said "mutation micOpened's reply was refused: ... NotAllowedError; its slot keeps what it had and its `then` does not run". The rule is in the error-alert recipe, not in the permissions recipe. Fixed with a derive of the step's `failure(...)` and a task on it. One drive and about 10 minutes. What would have helped: one line in the permissions recipe's "Recover" saying a refused request runs no `then` and to read `failure(x)`.
- `[lower-key-position]` warns for a list of plain numbers (a waveform's bars, drawn with `for b, i in bars key=i`): the position is the bars' only identity (fixed length, repeated values). Three or four warnings in every build and drive, with no way to say the position is meant, and `verify ios` counts them as problems ("verify: 4 problems (4 findings; the tests passed)"). Workaround for a clean run: the data module turns each number into a record whose id is its place, and the loop keys by that id: the same key, no warning. About 12 minutes in all.
- `audio-waveform`'s `tint` takes only a hex: the package's Swift parses a hex literal, so a colour role or token is dropped without a warning. Wrote `tint="#ff3b30"` (light red, also in dark mode). About 3 minutes, reading the package's Swift.
- The recorder writes `audio/mp4` or `audio/wav` on Apple; a chat network's voice note is Ogg Opus. The app's native module converts each recording itself (AVAssetReader, AudioToolbox's Opus encoder, an Ogg page writer with its CRC, and a waveform of the whole take): about 200 lines, about 45 minutes with checking. What would have helped: `audio/ogg` (Opus) as a recorder type, and a finished take's waveform.
- Under `--touch platform` (needed for a swipe), `tap room-<id>` on a chat list's `link` row failed: "the touch landed on node #16, not on node #14 the aim hit-tested". A tap on the row's text worked. About 3 minutes (a known finding).
- A long-press drive with a multi-word label target (`tap 'Voice message, 0:07' contextmenu`) was refused with "unknown word"; the message named the quoting to use. Under a minute; noted only because single quotes inside the op are the shell's habit.
- `tap @9 allow` once answered "not pending: @9" right after the request; with `state pending` before it, `@9` worked, and so did `@micOpened` (the mutation's name). Not isolated. About 3 minutes.

### Lean in
- `@exact/recording`'s Apple implementation documents what it writes in its header (AAC in a fragmented MP4 by default, WAV under the agent, one-second fragments), so the format a sender must convert was clear before writing any code.
- The whole record flow (the microphone, the recorder, its live level view, stop, the file) worked in its first drive: under the agent the recorder writes a tone that follows a synthetic level, so a drive records, stops, plays the take and sends it with no microphone. `--permission microphone=prompt` with the ticket's `allow` and `deny` tested both paths.
- A hidden `audio` with bound `paused`, `playbackRate`, `preservesPitch` and `timeupdate` gave a custom player on the first drive; switching its `src` stops one message and plays the next; `state media` shows the rate, the time and the file.
- `state voiceSpeed = 1 persist` kept one speed across launches with one word, in a `members` file.
- A contract-only edit ran on the existing iOS build ("running the new plan on the existing build"): each look fix took seconds, not a native rebuild.
- `tap <label> contextmenu` on a node inside the long-press target reached its ancestor's handler, so a drive can hold a message by its label when its id changes between launches.
- `verify ios` (about 50 s) named a button inside another button (a still copy of the bubble inside a preview that is a button, behind an `if` the compiler cannot see through) and one fixed glyph size, each with its fix; the next run was clean.
