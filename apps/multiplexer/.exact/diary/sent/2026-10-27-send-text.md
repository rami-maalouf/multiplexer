### Rough
- After `tap discard-<id>` (a mutation whose effect arrives later, when the module calls `context.changed` and the resource is asked again), a drive had no way to wait for the row to go away: `clock data` lands the mutation's reply, not the later push, and `clock until state <path> == <literal>` has only `==`, so "until this message is gone" or "until the state is no longer X" cannot be written. Used `"clock +2000 real"` and `"clock +3000 real"`. ~3 min, and two fixed real-time waits that the docs say never to write. What would have helped: `clock until state <path> != <literal>`, or `clock until tree missing <testId> within <ms>` (see also the finding that a drive cannot wait for a module's own work).

- The `chat-thread` recipe's composer, copied as written, failed `check` on iOS: "composer [25,809 325×20]: 325×20 pt, under the 44×44 minimum". The `textarea` is one line tall inside the row's 4-point padding. Fixed in the app: the row's vertical padding moved into the field (`padding="12px 0"` on the `textarea`, `margin="4px 0"` on Send), so the field is 44 points tall and the composer keeps its height. ~5 min. What would have helped: the recipe passing `check` as written (the recipe's `overflow-wrap="anywhere"` also fails `verify`, an earlier finding).
