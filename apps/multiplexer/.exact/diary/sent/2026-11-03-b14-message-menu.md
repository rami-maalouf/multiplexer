### Rough
- A sheet route whose content is a component (`if e.name == "x"` → `column navigationKey=… navigationPresentation="modal"` → `MySheet(...)`, whose root `column` holds the `header`) warned `lower-header-nested`: the header is not the route's first child, so it is not the navigation bar. The message named the fix (move `navigationKey` and the other navigation props onto the component's root, passing the route id as a prop), which worked at once. But the edit-sheet recipe nests its sheet the same way (route `column` → `for` → component → `column` → `header`) and compiles clean, so I had copied a shape that the recipe says is fine. About 5 minutes. What would have helped: the recipe and the rule agreeing (the recipe puts the navigation props on the component's root, or the rule accepts the recipe's shape for both).
- A `for` inside a `virtualized=true` list with an `if`/`else` as the row (a title row or a row of cells) is refused: "a `for` in a virtualized list needs exactly one element flow root (wrap conditional or multiple roots in column)". Clear and quick (1 minute); noted because the same shape in a plain `list` is fine.
- `icon(sf "face.smiling")` without an `svg` in `app.tamber` is an error (`tamber-icon-missing`), because the web draws icons only from SVG, though the theme recipe shows `icon.play = icon(sf "play.fill")` alone. The message was excellent: it named the role that draws the same symbol everywhere (`symbol:emoji` for `face.smiling`) or `symbol:sf/<name>` with one warning. About 2 minutes.

- An `input type="search"` in a sheet's content (not in its `header`) draws its magnifier over its own placeholder at the accessibility text sizes ("🔍earch"), on the display, not only in the driver's drawing. At the default size the text sits clear of the icon. Capping its `font-size` did not help; `padding-left=40` moves the text past the icon there, but at the default size the padding adds to the field's own inset, so it is only written for `exactViewport().accessibilityTextSize`. About 15 minutes and four drives. What would have helped: the field's text inset growing with its icon, as UIKit's search field does.
- A search field in a sheet's `header` is UIKit's search controller, which hides the sheet's navigation bar while the person searches, and the bar's Send button with it: a drive could check a row, then not tap Send ("ScrollView covers its middle"). Moving the field into the sheet's content kept the bar. The search vocab card tells how to keep a field shown while scrolling (`position="sticky"`), not how to keep the bar while searching. About 10 minutes. What would have helped: a line in the search card ("in a sheet with a Save or Send in its bar, the bar hides while searching; put the field in the content, or …"), or a prop for `hidesNavigationBarDuringPresentation`. Also: in an iOS 26 half-height sheet, the bar's search field sits at the sheet's bottom, under the content's own bottom row (a tab strip), which the docs do not say.
- `screenshot <png> window` over the app-drawn overlay and in sheets almost always fell back to `pixels="app"` ("the display changed while it was read", or "the app named no points to check the display against"), so glass and blurs were approximate. With `--timing platform`, `clock +3000`, `clock settle` and two tries it still fell back. What worked: holding the drive with `clock until state <path> == "never" within 12000` (which waits in real time, then fails the drive) and running `xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot` from a second shell meanwhile. A `clock +N` under `--timing platform` did not hold the app in real time (the capture came after the app had closed). About 25 minutes over the task. What would have helped: a `screenshot … window` that waits for a still display, or a `hold <ms>` step that keeps the app up in real time.
- `tap emoji-grid drag 0 -400` on a virtualized list in a half-height sheet aimed at (201, 855), under the tab row below the list, and was refused ("node … covers its middle"); `layout emoji-grid` said the list's frame was y 522 to 782 on screen. A drag that starts on a button in the list is refused without `--touch platform` ("starts on nothing the agent scrolls"); with `--touch platform` it pulled the sheet up. About 6 minutes. What would have helped: aiming at the visible part of the list's frame.
- In a half-height sheet the route's box ends about 30 points above the sheet's visible bottom edge, and the virtualized grid drew its rows past its frame into that strip, below the tab row that follows it (seen on the display). A negative `margin-bottom` with matching `padding-bottom` on the tab row covered the strip. Evidence for the known finding about rows drawn past a virtualized list's frame. About 10 minutes.
- `clock 600` after `clock +2500`-style steps: "the clock cannot go backwards (2500.0 → 600.0)". Clear, but it could have said that `clock +600` advances by 600. 1 minute.

- A drive after `verify ios` opened signed out: `verify`'s tests start with an empty store but the drives' Keychain item, so the native module saw a session without its store and signed it out (as it must after a reinstall). The next drive needed a new sign-in, and every message id changed (they come from the new store's timeline). About 5 minutes. Evidence for the known finding that a native module has no secret store of Exact's that a fresh drive or test empties with the rest.
- `check both` on an app-drawn long-press overlay reports its full-screen dismiss layer as "covered: its middle is under menu-forward at rest". The layer is under the whole overlay on purpose (a tap anywhere outside closes the menu); with a two-row menu its middle was free, with a seven-row menu it is not. Not fixed. Evidence for the known findings about `check` and app-drawn overlays and bars.
