### Rough
- `contract vocab header` says a pressable title exists on the Mac ("a pressable title ... is drawn in the page") but not what it does on iOS, and `contract vocab menu` says only that a menu button "in a `header`" is the bar item's pull-down. Whether the heading itself can open a menu (UIKit's `titleMenuProvider`) is answered only by a draft RFC, LLP 1141.003 D3, which designs it and is not built; what the host does today (a button around the heading becomes a drawn `HeaderTitleView`, whose tap presses that button) took reading `NavigationTitleIOS.swift`. Cost about 10 minutes. What would have helped: one line in the header card on what a button around the heading does on iOS today, and whether its `popovertarget` opens the menu.
- A `button popovertarget="title-menu"` around the header's `text role="heading"` (the form LLP 1141.003 calls "today's title press") builds with `lower-header-no-bar`: "this route's `header` holds no heading, so on iOS it is not the navigation bar". The heading is there, one box down, inside the button; the iOS host's `HeaderTitle` walks into a pressable wrapper, but the compiler's walk does not. The message lists ways out, none of them a title that opens a menu. Cost about 5 minutes. What would have helped: the warning naming the heading it found inside the button and saying whether a pressable title is supported, or the title menu itself.
- A `row press=openTitleMenu` around the heading builds clean and makes the title a drawn `HeaderTitleView`, but on iOS 26 it draws the title twice: a centred inline "Chats" in the bar above the large title "Chats" (`.exact/b21/inbox.png`). Its press runs, and `showPopover("title-menu", source="title-button")` is logged (`command showPopover("title-menu", "title-button")`), yet no menu shows, with the menu column in the header or in the route beside it, and nothing says why. Cost about 15 minutes over three tries. What I did instead: the route draws its own top row (`navigationBar="none"`) holding a `button popovertarget` with the title's words and a chevron, which is UIKit's pull-down from a page button. What would have helped: the title menu of LLP 1141.003 D3, or until then a warning that a popover shown from the bar's title (or with a `source` that is in the bar) cannot show, and no second inline title for a pressable large title.
- A menu's `button disabled=true` with no `press` (a feature that comes later, shown greyed) was left out of UIKit's pull-down: the menu showed Settings alone. The iOS host turns a row with no press handler into a section break (`MenusIOS.swift`, `items(of:)`), and `disabled` is applied only to a row that presses. `contract vocab menu` says "`destructive=true` paints a row red" and "An `hr` between rows starts a section", but not that a row with no press is a break too, and the compiler said nothing. Found by reading the host's source; cost about 5 minutes. Workaround: the greyed rows say `press=settings`, which a disabled row never runs. What would have helped: a disabled row kept as a dimmed action whatever its handlers, or a warning ("this menu row has no press, so iOS drops it").
