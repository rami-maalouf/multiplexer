# Soft edges that are not a black shelf

## Rough

- Window `screenshot` often falls back to the app's own drawing while the display
  changes, so glass and backdrop materials look solid even when the display shot is
  fine. Cost about ten minutes of false "still black" reads until a longer real
  settle (`clock +1500 real` and up) returned `pixels="display"`. A note in the
  screenshot op's reply already says this; a one-line "retry once for display
  pixels" in the agent help would have saved the loop.

## Lean in

- The home bottom bar's recipe (one material, percentage fade, `chat.edge-bottom`)
  was a clear reference the product owner named; matching the composer to it was
  a straight copy.

## Needed

- Nothing new for this fix.

## Checkpoints

- Composer edge = home bottom bar.
- Chat top: bar at window y 0 (`top=-62`), controls at y 62, soft edge
  (ultra-thin + `chat.edge-top`) starts part-clear under the clock; transcript
  runs under the status bar.
- Inbox top: same lighter recipe, peaks around the title, disperses down.
- Simulator display shots under `.exact/edge-fix/` (`*-v9`).
- Both tops = the bottom bar's recipe mirrored (dark material in dark, chat.edge-bottom,
  full for 40% then one straight fade); inbox top always drawn. Display shots `*-v11`:
  status band is one smooth ramp in dark and light.
- Less blur under the status bar only (mask 0.7 at the top, full by 40%): `*-v12`. It made a
  dark line where the mask peaked (app-side, not the framework). Fixed with a mask that only
  falls: 85% flat to 40%, then fades (`line-b*`).

## Rough (status-bar shelf)

- A chat route with `navigationBar="none"` and a zero-height box above its scroller
  starts under the status bar; the bar's soft edge at `top=-40` left the page's
  colour as a hard shelf above the clock. `layout chat-bar` showed the bar at
  window y 62 even after removing the zero-height box, so a negative top on the
  material alone was not enough: the bar itself needs `top=-62` (with the
  controls padded back) and the transcript `margin-top=-62` so messages run under
  the clock. About 25 minutes across layout probes and false "still black" reads
  of window screenshots. A safe-area inset prop inside a route (finding 53) would
  have named the reach.

## Rough (voice follow-up)

- Starting a voice note used to `blur("composer")` and also unmount the field, so the
  keyboard always fell even with `retainFocus` on the voice button. Keeping the field
  mounted (hidden) and focusing it instead matches Beeper; about 10 minutes to see that
  unmounting was the second cause.

## Rough (keyboard mid-scroll + glass stop)

- `buttonStyle="glass"` refuses `background-color` (`lower-button-style`): a tinted liquid-
  glass Stop needs `appearance="none"` plus `backgroundMaterial="glass"`. The vocab error
  named the rule; about 2 minutes. A short note on the glass button card that a tinted glass
  circle uses that pair would have saved the try.
- A drive's microphone permission holds `clock settle` on a ticket; without
  `--permission microphone=granted` the recorder stays `opening` and Stop stays disabled.
  Known from the voice diary; another ~5 minutes this pass until the flag was used.

## Rough (a faint line at the status bar's foot)

- After the app's own top edges were smooth, a faint dark line stayed at y 62 on both screens.
  Cause: the platform's automatic scroll edge effect on each scroller, drawn under the status bar
  on a route with no bar, ending at its foot. `layout room-list native` named it
  (`edge-effect of #3`); `-exact-scroll-edge-effect="none"` on the list and the transcript removed
  it (screenshot diff only in rows 0 to 62; bottom edges identical). `contract vocab
  scroll-edge-effect` said "not a built-in tag or attribute", so the name came from grepping an
  example app; about 20 minutes with the host source read. A vocab hit for the bare name, and a
  line in the `navigationBar="none"` docs, would have saved it.
- Eased top fade (flat to 30%, then smoothstep stops), material and tint on one curve, so no
  corner in the slope reads as a dark line (`line-d*`). App-side.

## Rough (interactive glass)

- The title capsule and the field's glass did not respond to a held finger as interactive glass
  does. The host sets interactive glass only when the glass node itself has `press`; this came
  from reading the host source, not the docs. A `press` on each glass node (innermost wins, so it
  fires once) plus `-exact-press-opacity=1` fixed it. The agent's held tap showed no change, so
  only a device can show the effect. About 15 minutes. (Finding 135.)
