### Rough
- A small symbol right after a list row's title, as a "muted" mark (a crossed bell after a chat's name). In a `row` beside the title `text`, it sits after a one-line title, but when the title wraps it goes to the right edge of the title's box, not after its last word (as CSS does), and at the largest text size it squeezed the title until a word spilled (`check`: "its text is 5 pt wider than its box"). A `flex-wrap` row fixed the spill but put the symbol on a line of its own. To keep it after the last word I put it inside the `text` (`text` > `text name` + `image "symbol:..." font="-exact-footnote"`). The build took that with no error and no warning, and on iOS the symbol was not drawn at all. Searching exact2 found the closed issue for images in a paragraph (LLP 1011.002); its spec says an inline image with no `width` or `height` takes its natural size, "else zero". With `width="0.8em" height="0.8em" vertical-align="baseline"` the symbol shows after the last word, at every text size, as wanted. Cost about 20 minutes (three builds and drives, and the LLP). What would have helped: a warning when a `symbol:` image inside a `text` has no size (it draws at zero size), and a line in the symbols recipe: "a symbol inside a text, after its words: give it `width` and `height` in `em`". The excord sample has the form only for a raster emoji.
- At the largest text size, a long-press menu (a `contextPopover` column) is taller than the screen. Under `--touch platform`, `tap menu-delete` (the last row, below the fold of UIKit's scrolling menu) answered "the menu of popover #245 (chat-menu) does not show its rows: tap its invoker first, then `clock settle`", though the menu was open. Without the flag, `tap room-x contextmenu` then `tap menu-delete` answered "its middle is outside the viewport; scroll it into view first", with no way named to scroll a menu. So no drive reached the confirmation alert at that size. Cost about 6 minutes. What would have helped: a tap on a menu row that scrolls the menu to it, as `tap` does for a list row, or the message naming the menu's scroll.
